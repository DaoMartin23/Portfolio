Personal Informatics App

A web app that tracks sleep, screen time and stress, built over two months by a ten-person team at the University of Bath. I led the Scrum planning.

  • Agile/Scrum
  • Full stack
  • Team of 10
University team project · code not public

Overview

The brief was a personal informatics tool: something that lets a person log their own sleep, screen time and stress, then look back and see how the three move together over time. Ten of us built it as a full-stack web app in first year, over two months, with each person owning a component of the system.

My part

I ran the Scrum side. That meant keeping the product backlog in order, planning each sprint, keeping the sprint tracker and burndown charts up to date, and running the Scrum meetings. When a technical blocker or a requirement change came up, I re-planned the sprint instead of letting the schedule slip and hoping.

How the team shipped

Each member developed their component independently and everything came together through GitHub. Integration problems were dealt with as branches merged, rather than saved up for the end, which is the part I would push harder on next time.

What I took from it

The burndown chart showed when we were behind days before anyone said so out loud. Re-planning early cost less than re-planning late, and that habit has carried into how I run my own projects.
