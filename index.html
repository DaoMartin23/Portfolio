All About Me

I'm a second-year Computer Science student at the University of Bath, on the placement course, with a first predicted after year one. I write Python for things that need to be right. The last two were a lap by lap F1 race simulator with a seeded engine, and a Fantasy Premier League assistant that trains Random Forest models and answers questions through a LangChain agent.

Both projects have a front end now, one in React and TypeScript, one in Streamlit, because I would rather learn the whole stack than hand the interesting half to someone else. At uni I ran the Scrum side of a ten-person team project, and I still lose Sunday afternoons to F1. I'm looking for a placement year.
